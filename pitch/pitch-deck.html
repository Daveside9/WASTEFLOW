The Problem

Nigeria's Waterways Are Drowning in Waste

Marine plastic and water hyacinth are choking Nigeria's 853 km coastline and thousands of km of inland waterways — disrupting navigation, fishing, and coastal livelihoods.

🚫

No Visibility

Communities can't see where waste is accumulating until it's a crisis

📭

No Coordination

Collectors, recyclers, and hubs operate in silos with no shared platform

🔓

No Trust Layer

Unverified weight claims mean buyers and sellers can't agree on value

💸

No Incentives

Community participation dries up without clear, transparent rewards

🔗

No Market Link

Collected material rarely reaches recyclers — the circular loop is broken

📊

No Data

Without records, hotspots repeat and government response is reactive

AI Waste Detection

Real Weather. Real Risk. Live Alerts.

The AI engine monitors 18 Nigerian waterways using live weather from Open-Meteo (free, no API key) and scores each for waste accumulation risk.

Live Data Inputs

  • Rainfall (mm/hour)
  • Wind speed (km/h)
  • Wind direction (compass)
  • Relative humidity (%)

AI Scoring Rules

  • Rainfall >10mm → +35 pts
  • Wind >30 km/h → +25 pts
  • Onshore wind → +15 pts
  • Historical zone → +10 pts

Product Features

Everything in One Platform

🗺️

Live AI Waste Map

Pulsing red dots on Nigeria map. Real weather from Open-Meteo. One-tap listing from any alert.

⚖️

Verified Trade Flow

6-stage pipeline with auditable timeline. Weigher verification as the trust gate before any sale.

🔵

Blue Credits

5 credits per verified kg. Transparent calculation. Full transaction history per listing.

📊

Impact Dashboard

Verified kg collected, active hubs, materials transferred. Reported ≠ verified — always clear.

🔔

Role-Targeted Alerts

Weighers get listing alerts. Buyers get verification alerts. Sellers track their own pipeline.

📱

Fully Responsive

Mobile-first design. Works on all screen sizes. Dark and light mode. Accessible forms.
